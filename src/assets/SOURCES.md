## License rules

- **CC0** (public domain): use freely, no attribution required (but record here anyway).
- **CC-BY**: attribution required — credit will go in in-game credits screen and `CREDITS.md`.
- **CC-BY-SA**: avoid (forces our project to be share-alike).
- **CC-BY-NC**: avoid (we are commercial).
- **Unknown**: do not use until verified.

## Recommended sources

- https://kenney.nl — CC0 3D models, audio, UI
- https://opengameart.org — mixed licenses, check each asset
- https://freesound.org — audio, mostly CC-BY or CC0
- https://www.fab.com — Unreal/free assets
- https://sketchfab.com — filter "Free + CC license"
